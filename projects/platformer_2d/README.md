# 2D Platformer

A classic 2D platformer game made with VisualGasic for Godot 4.5.

## How to Play

1. Open this folder as a Godot project
2. Run the main scene (main.tscn)

## Controls

- **Left/Right Arrows**: Move left/right
- **Space**: Jump

## Gameplay

- Jump across platforms to reach the golden end platform
- Collect coins for bonus points (100 points each)
- Avoid or defeat enemies
- Jump on enemies' heads to defeat them (200 points each)
- You have 3 lives - Game Over when all lives are lost
- Fall off the screen and you lose a life
- Touch an enemy from the side and you lose a life
- Press Space after Game Over or completing the level to play again

## Scoring

- Coin collected: 100 points
- Enemy defeated: 200 points

## Level Features

- 6 platforms of varying heights
- 10 coins to collect
- 3 patrolling enemies
- Camera follows the player horizontally
- Golden goal platform at the end

## Tips

- Take your time on difficult jumps
- Jump on enemies from above to defeat them safely
- Collect all coins for maximum score
- The golden platform at the end marks the level completion

Classic platforming fun!
