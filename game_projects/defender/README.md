# Defender Clone

A Defender-style side-scrolling space shooter made with VisualGasic for Godot 4.5.

## How to Play

1. Open this folder as a Godot project
2. Run the main scene (main.tscn)

## Controls

- **Arrow Keys**: Move ship up/down/left/right
- **Space**: Shoot

## Gameplay

- Defend your ship from waves of enemies
- Enemies will chase you and shoot back
- The world scrolls horizontally - you can move left and right across a large playfield
- Destroy enemies to earn points
- You have 3 lives - Game Over when all lives are lost
- Press Space after Game Over to restart

## Features

- Side-scrolling camera that follows the player
- Enemy AI that pursues the player
- Bullet management for both player and enemies
- Starfield parallax background
- Score and lives tracking
- Wave-based enemy spawning

Enjoy defending your sector!
