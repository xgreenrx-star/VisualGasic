Building a Kenney-styled platformer from the Godot First 2D Game tutorial patterns.

**generated/platformer/assets/kenney/** — CC0 sprites (grass, brick, alienGreen, coinGold, slimeGreen) from kenney.nl.

**game.vg** — ASCII level loader, tile collision, coins (+100 score), patrolling slime, autopilot jumps at gaps/walls, `LoadPicture` + `DrawTextureRect`.

**main.tscn** — Node2D + game.vg.

Apply to preview — player should run, jump platforms, and collect coins.
