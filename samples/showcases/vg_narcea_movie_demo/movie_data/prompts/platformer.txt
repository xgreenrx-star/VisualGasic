Create a 2D platformer in Visual Gasic with Kenney Platformer Pack Redux graphics (CC0). Tile-based level with grass/brick platforms, jumping player alien, collectible gold coins, patrolling slime enemy, score HUD, and scrolling camera. Download sprites from https://kenney.nl/assets/platformer-pack-redux into generated/platformer/assets/kenney/. Save game.vg + main.tscn.
