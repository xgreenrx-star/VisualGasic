Brotato 3D — keyboard controls
================================

Movement
  W / Up arrow     Move forward (world +Z)
  S / Down arrow   Move back
  A / Left arrow   Move left
  D / Right arrow  Move right

Combat
  (none)           Weapons auto-fire at the nearest enemy in range

Camera — free look (default)
  Q                Orbit yaw left
  E                Orbit yaw right
  R                Pitch up (zoom angle)
  F                Pitch down

Camera — chase mode (press C to toggle)
  C                Toggle chase / free look
  (in chase)       Camera yaw follows your movement direction
  Q E R F          Disabled in chase mode (pitch locked)

Other
  Esc              Pause / resume

Notes
  - Start on the title screen: pick a character, then Start game.
  - Shop between waves: click items, then Continue.
