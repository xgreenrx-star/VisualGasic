## Post copy (paste into Facebook)

Visual Gasic is heading toward **6.0 stable** early next year. Before we tag that release, I want one thing on the table that proves the language isn’t a tech demo: **a real game people want to finish.**

Working title: **GRAVEN.**

It’s a side-view cave explorer inspired by the old BBC Micro games where gravity pulls toward *mass*, not always toward the floor. You thrust a little prospector pod through rock, flip your thinking when “down” changes, push crates, and fire a sonar pulse through dark rooms when the lights go out. Eight rooms for the first slice. About fifteen minutes. Tight on purpose.

The whole point for us: **game logic written in Visual Gasic** — VB6-style `.vg` files you can actually read — running on Godot 4.6. When you clear the slice, the screen says **Made in VG6**. That’s the sales pitch for the language, and I’d rather show it with a game than another benchmark chart.

**Who I’m looking for**

Someone who grew up on **VB6** (or VBA) and isn’t scared of modules and `Sub`/`Function` logic — Godot experience is a plus, not a requirement on day one.

And/or someone who **makes graphics**: cave tiles, a tiny pod, moody palette, the room that goes almost black until sonar hits. That’s the screenshot we need.

You don’t have to be both. A logic person plus an art person works. One person who is both is gold.

**What’s already done**

Room layouts, physics targets, controls, and a full developer brief are written in the repo. Week one is “make gravity feel fun in room two.” If that lands, we keep going.

**What you get**

Credit on the game and in the repo. Paid terms negotiable depending on scope (volunteer / rev share / contract — open to talk). GPL-3.0 project, same as Visual Gasic.

If this sounds like the kind of scoped project you can finish in a month part-time — not a multi-year MMO — **comment or DM me** with:

• VB6 / VG comfort (1–10)  
• Godot comfort (1–10)  
• Art comfort (1–10)  
• Hours per week you could give for ~4 weeks  

I’ll send the full proposal and we can do a short call to open the project in Godot together.

Visual Gasic was built for people who want to **read** game code, not decode it. GRAVEN is the first time we ask a player to care about that on purpose. Help us make it count for 6.0 stable.
