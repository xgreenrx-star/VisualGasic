2) Scope of AI assistance

AI was used for:
- Drafting wording for narrative answers
- Rewriting content for clarity and concision
- Producing alternative versions for form fields with character limits
- Preparing plain-text attachment drafts (tasks/milestones and budget justification)

AI was not used to:
- Invent project history, credentials, or funding status
- Make independent budget commitments
- Submit the form on my behalf
